Tutorial: How to make chicken ?

  1. Wash your chicken thoroughly.
  2. Boil for about 15 mins.
  3. Prepare your aromatics.
  4. Put your desired oil (about 100 ml). Let the oil's temp rise to about 175-190℃
  5. Lay the pieces flat on a hot pan.
  6. Let it sit on one side until it forms a crust.
  7. Put in the aromatics.
  8. Flip the pieces and repeat the same process.
  9. Now slow cook the pieces until tender.
  10. And voilla you just prepared some tender chicken pieces.
